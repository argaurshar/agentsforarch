# 3D site analysis
You are turning the top-down map or satellite image in the input into a 3D site analysis diagram: the site and its surroundings modelled on a circular isometric base, like a coin, isolated on white. The input is a screenshot, so it carries interface elements: map pins, the search bar, zoom controls, attribution and watermarks, road and place labels, and the cursor. Read them to understand the site, then remove every one of them from your output. None of that furniture appears in the finished image. STEP 1 — READ THE MAP FIRST. Find the centre of the site — the area outlined in red, or the centre of the image if nothing is outlined. Read the building footprints, their relative heights from shadows and roof detail, the streets, trees, parks and water within a radius around it. Only what lies inside the circle is drawn. STEP 2 — BUILD THE COIN. A circular base in isometric view, its edge shown as a thin slab. On it: buildings as simple extruded volumes in soft greys at plausible relative heights, streets as pale flat bands, trees as simple rounded sage-green shapes, water as pale blue. Everything outside the circle is plain white — no map, no ground, no shadow beyond the base. Muted, desaturated and diagrammatic: no photorealism, only soft, toned-down shadows. North points to the top right. The SECOND image is a reference diagram. Copy its layout, graphic language and annotation style — not its place. The geography comes only from the first image. STEP 3 — ANNOTATE: 1. N, E, S and W at the four ends of the base, clockwise, with N at the top right. 2. Sun path for latitude 27.2° N: a semi-transparent orange arc over the site, rising in the east, passing to the SOUTH of overhead, setting in the west, with a small sun icon at its highest point. Keep the site itself in a soft accent colour. The four compass letters are the only text. Do not add any watermark, signature, caption or stray text. CHECK before you finish: are N, E, S, W clockwise with N at the top right? Does the sun arc lean to the south? Is everything outside the circle white?
